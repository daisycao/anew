# Our pricing

My own project: we have to put a price on our app this month.

- Today: cost + margin = $24 a month. Nobody we interviewed would pay more than $10.
- Ford's order, from [[Model T — a car for everyone]]: start at the price people can pay, then ask what has to change to make it.
- What would have to be true to sell at $10? Fewer features? Self-serve only? Ten times the users?
- Ford's catch: it only works if volume can grow a lot. Can ours?
