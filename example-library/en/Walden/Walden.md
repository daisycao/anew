# Walden

## 1. Live deliberately #product

> I went to the woods because I wished to live deliberately. —— Thoreau, 1854

👩 He ran an experiment on his own life and wrote up the results.

## 2. Cut what isn't essential #business

👩 His accounts of beans and boards are half the book.

## 3. Time is a stream, not a ledger #product

> Time is but the stream I go a-fishing in. —— Thoreau, 1854

👩 The opposite of Franklin: time isn't spent, you fish in it.

> [!claude]
> A good pairing, though Thoreau kept careful accounts too. He's against wasting life on things, not against counting.

## 4. Reading hour: fewer, deeper books #for-me
