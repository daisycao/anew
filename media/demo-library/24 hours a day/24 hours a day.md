# 24 hours a day

## 1. Every day you get 24 new hours #fact

> No one can take it from you. It is unstealable. —— Bennett, 1910

👩 Nobody gets more. The rich don't, the busy don't.

## 2. Evenings are a second day #product

👩 (from my retell) The 9-to-5 is not the day. The hours after it are a day of their own, and I've been treating them as leftovers.

> [!claude]
> That's his central move: treat the hours after work as a day of their own, and plan that day on purpose.

## 3. Reading hour: 90 minutes, three evenings a week #for-me

👩 Start small: Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday.

> [!claude]
> This is close to his actual prescription, so you're not stretching him. He also warns against taking on too much at the start.
